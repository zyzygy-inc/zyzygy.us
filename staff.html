Our team

The people behind Zyzygy, Inc.

Jim Thornsberry

Jim Thornsberry has been writing software for over 40 years, most of it in Python, C and C++.

Today Jim works from Goldthwaite, in Mills County, Texas. Zyzygy, Inc. is the company he runs to do two things he enjoys: helping businesses with practical software, and making small apps for families.

Jim likes work that is careful and plain: code that does what it says, has tests, and is easy for the next person to read. He'll tell you if a job is smaller than you think, and he won't sell you something you don't need.

Experience and credentials

  • Over 40 years of writing software. Mostly in Python, C and C++.
  • Industry experience. High-Performance Computing & High-Performance Storage; Finance & Trading; Defense: Situational Awareness/Battlespace Management, VHF Networking, Software-Defined Radio; Plant Floor Automation; Life Insurance & Annuities; Health Insurance.
  • Security clearance. Jim previously held a Secret clearance.
  • Hands-on work. Python automation, tools and back ends; C and C++ libraries, performance and memory work; legacy modernization; code audits, tests and CI.
