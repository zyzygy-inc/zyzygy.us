Kitty's Squeak & Splash privacy policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

We collect nothing.

The short version: Kitty's Squeak & Splash doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The few things the app remembers stay on your tablet.

What the app keeps on your tablet

  • Your settings: sound level, haptics, speed, timer and the other game options
  • Up to 3 cat names, only if you type them in (optional), with each cat's Paw Counter totals such as catches and best streak
  • Small reminders for the app itself, like whether you've seen the lock coach, whether to stay locked between games, and how many sessions have been played
  • A list of which purchases you own, so they work even when the store can't be reached

Settings and cats are kept in one small save file in the app's own storage, plus a backup copy of the previous save so a crash can't wipe them. If a save file is ever damaged, the app sets it aside on the tablet instead of erasing it. The list of purchases is kept separately in the app's own storage. The app never sends these files anywhere (your own device backup is covered under Backups below). Zyzygy, Inc. never receives them. We have no server and no copy of them.

Apple or Google stays the real record of what you've bought. The app checks with them and updates its list.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ever, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No subscriptions.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

When the app uses the internet

Kitty's Squeak & Splash works offline, and the app itself never connects to the internet. When you buy, restore or redeem a code for a purchase (the Holiday Pack or an add-on game), Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles it under their own privacy policies, and the store needs a network connection to do that. We never see payment details.

There's no rate-this-app prompt.

Who it's for

Kitty's Squeak & Splash is made for cats and the adults who look after them. It isn't directed at children. Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Backups

If you use Android's own backup, your tablet may include the app's save file and its backup copy (nothing else) in your personal Google account backup. On iPad and iPhone, your own iCloud Backup or computer backup may include it in the same way. Those backups belong to you and are covered by Google's or Apple's privacy policies. Zyzygy never receives them and can't see them.

Deleting information

In Settings, Reset cat names and totals removes every cat name, paw count and title on the tablet (settings and purchases stay). The backup copy of the previous save may still hold them until the app's next save replaces it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the tablet, including the backup copy. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it 2 years after your question is resolved.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices ever change, we'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released. Questions: support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days. We answer by email only; we don't take questions by mail. Zyzygy, Inc., Goldthwaite, Texas.
