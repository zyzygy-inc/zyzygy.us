Reward Buddy privacy policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version: Reward Buddy doesn't collect any personal information from children or grown-ups. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your family's tablet.

What the app keeps on your tablet

  • Each child's first name (or nickname), picture, color and buddy, typed in by a grown-up
  • Points, chore history, earned certificates and grown-up requests
  • Your chores, rewards and settings
  • The grown-up PIN, stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value, never as the number itself
  • Your purchase status, read from Apple or Google

All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your tablet: one save file, plus a backup copy of the previous save so a crash can't wipe your kids' points or the PIN. If a save file is ever damaged, the app sets the damaged copy aside on the tablet instead of erasing it. The app never sends these files anywhere (your own device backup is covered under Backups below). Zyzygy, Inc. never receives them. We have no server and no copy of them.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Reward Buddy is made for children ages 2 to 8 and their families. We don't collect, use or share personal information from children. Nothing a child does in the app is sent anywhere. There's nothing for a child to sign up for, no chat, and no way for a child to post anything.

Because we don't collect children's information, there's nothing we hold to review or delete. If you think a child has emailed us, tell us and we'll delete it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ever, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

A grown-up gate

Purchases, Restore Purchases, settings, sharing and printing sit behind a 4-digit grown-up PIN. The app doesn't link out to websites. Grown-up request alerts are local notifications on the same tablet.

When the app uses the internet

Reward Buddy works offline. It uses the internet only when a grown-up buys or restores an upgrade. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles that under their own privacy policies. We never see payment details.

Jokes read aloud

The buddy reads jokes aloud with the tablet's built-in text-to-speech. On iPad and iPhone, it uses Apple's speech voices on the device. On Android, jokes are read aloud only with a voice that's installed on the tablet and works without the internet (an on-device voice from the tablet's text-to-speech engine). The app never uses an online voice. If the tablet has no on-device voice, the joke shows as text, and the grown-up area explains how to download one. The app only ever gives it the joke text, never a child's name. You can turn off Speak jokes aloud in Settings; the joke still shows as text.

Sharing and printing certificates

A grown-up can share a certificate as a picture or print it (printing needs Plus). A certificate shows the child's name, the reward and the date. Printing uses your tablet's own print dialog and goes to your printer over your home network. Sharing uses your tablet's share menu and goes only where you send it. Zyzygy doesn't receive either.

Backups

If your family uses Android's own backup, your tablet may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup. On Android this is only the save file and its backup copy (kids, points, walls and the scrambled PIN), never test settings. On iPad and iPhone, your family's own iCloud Backup or computer backup may include it in the same way. Those backups belong to you and are covered by Google's or Apple's privacy policies. Zyzygy never receives them and can't see them.

Deleting information

In the grown-up area you can delete a child (with their points, history and wall) or a single certificate. The app's backup copy of the previous save may still hold that information until the app's next save replaces it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the tablet, including the backup copy. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it 2 years after your question is resolved.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices ever change, we'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and we won't start collecting children's information without first meeting COPPA's parental-notice and consent rules. Parents with questions: support@zyzygy.us. We answer by email only; we don't take questions by mail. Zyzygy, Inc., Goldthwaite, Texas.
