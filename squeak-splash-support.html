Kitty's Fun Emporium support

Help for cat owners. Email support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days.

Locking the tablet to the app

How do I keep my cat in the game?

Use your tablet's own lock. The app's Paw-proof setup (on the main menu) walks you through it and shows a live "Locked" check. iPad and iPhone: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) and set a passcode, then come back to the app, triple-click the top button (iPad) or side button (iPhone), or the Home button on older devices, and tap Start. To end it, triple-click again on iOS 26 and later, or double-click on iOS 17 and 18, then enter your passcode. The coach shows the right one for your device. Android: tap Lock to Kitty's Fun Emporium in the coach and confirm Android's "Pin app?" message. If nothing happens, turn on App pinning first (often Settings › Security › Advanced › App pinning; menus differ by maker).

Which Guided Access options should I pick?

Touch on, Motion off (stops the screen rotating), side/top button and volume buttons off, and a passcode or Face ID / Touch ID.

On Android, can my cat unpin it?

Possibly, with a lucky swipe-and-hold. Turn on Ask for PIN before unpinning (Android may call it Lock device when unpinning), and the tablet will ask for your PIN first. The app stays pinned between games, so you only pin once per play time. Tap Done playing (unlock) on the main menu to unpin, or use Android's own unpin gesture (the coach shows it for your tablet).

Do I have to lock it?

No, the games play either way. Without the lock the app still hides the system bars and ignores Back, but a determined paw can swipe home.

Getting out of a game

How do I stop a game?

Press and hold the dim paw in the top-left corner (top-right in right-to-left languages) for 3 seconds with one finger. A ring fills. Then tap the three numbers shown, in order. You'll get Resume, End session and speed controls. If you don't touch anything for 20 seconds, the game carries on.

The ring keeps resetting.

It only counts with exactly one still finger on the screen. If the cat is touching the screen too, wait for a paw-free moment, or lift the cat (good luck).

I don't want an exit corner at all.

On iPad and iPhone, open Guided Access's Options and switch off Grown-up exit corner. The corner disappears, and the only way out is Guided Access's own exit: triple-click on iOS 26 and later, double-click on iOS 17 and 18, then your passcode.

The game paused by itself.

The game pauses if the app is left (for example, a swipe home) and when the battery drops to 15% or less and isn't charging. When the timer runs out, it shows All done! Starting again needs the grown-up exit, so your cat can't press Play again.

Paws and play

My cat's taps don't seem to count.

Some paws (very dry or very furry) don't register well on glass. Try Catch helper on Easy, Prey size on Large, and a slower speed on the play screen. In Settings, turn on Nudge: near misses make the prey startle and dart, so your cat still gets a reaction.

My cat just lies on the tablet.

Classic loaf. Big contacts like a belly or tail never count as catches, and if your cat settles in, the prey scatter to the other side of the screen to tempt them up.

I can't hear any sound on my iPhone.

Open Settings in the app and check that Play sound even on silent is on. It's on by default, so the game plays even when the iPhone's Ring/Silent switch is on silent. If it's off, the silent switch mutes the game. Also check the sound level (Soft or Normal) and the device volume.

My cat lost interest.

That's normal. Try the other game, a different speed or fewer prey, and keep sessions short. Most cats enjoy a few short sessions a day.

What's the Paw Counter?

An optional score for the humans: catches, best streak and time played, plus a fun title at the end. It's off until you turn it on in Settings, and it stays on the tablet.

Will claws scratch my screen?

Lay the tablet flat on the floor, keep claws trimmed, and use a tempered-glass screen protector and a case. Wipe the screen afterwards. Stay nearby while your cat plays.

Purchases

What does it cost?

Pricing isn't final yet. We'll post it here before the app is in the stores. There will never be ads or subscriptions.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase is one time. No subscriptions, no ads, nothing to buy inside a game.

My purchase didn't unlock.

On the main menu, open More games & holidays and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the account you bought with. Apple and Google handle payments and refunds; we never see your payment details.

How do I redeem a code?

Open Settings and tap Redeem a promo code. On iPad and iPhone, pass the quick three-number check, then enter your code in the App Store sheet. On Android, it opens Google Play's redeem page; if the app is pinned, tap Done playing (unlock) on the main menu first. Whatever you redeem unlocks in the app automatically. If it doesn't show up, tap Restore purchases.

Does Family Sharing work?

On iPhone and iPad, yes: people in your Apple Family Sharing group get your purchases too. It never shares any data. On Android, purchases belong to the Google account that bought them and aren't shared with family.

A new tablet, or more than one

How do I delete my cats' names and scores?

Open Settings and tap Reset cat names and totals, then confirm. It removes every cat name, paw count and title on this tablet. Settings and purchases stay. To remove everything, delete the app.

We're getting a new tablet.

Purchases come back with Restore purchases. Settings, cat names and Paw Counter totals live on the old tablet; your own device backup (iCloud or Google) may carry them over, but we can't promise it.

Does anything ever leave the tablet?

Only through Apple or Google, never to us:

  • Apple or Google handles a purchase or restore.
  • Your tablet's own backup includes the app's data, if you have backup turned on.

Zyzygy has no server and never receives anything from the app.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your tablet (for example iPad 10th generation or Samsung Galaxy Tab), the app version, and what happened. A cat's name is optional. We reply within 2 business days.
