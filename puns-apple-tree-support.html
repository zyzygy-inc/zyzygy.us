Using the app

How do I have the book read aloud?

On the home screen, tap Read to Me. Each page is read aloud and turns on its own. Tap pause to stop for a moment.

How do I read it myself?

Tap Read Myself, then use Back and Next to turn the pages.

How do I play Punsapple Picking?

Tap the game button on the home screen, then tap an apple on the tree. The princess catches it and a pun from the story appears. There's no timer and no way to lose.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and to get updates. After that, it works offline.

Is it out yet?

Not yet. It's coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your phone or tablet, the app version if you can find it, and what happened. We reply within 2 business days.
