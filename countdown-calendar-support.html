Countdown Calendar support

We're a small company and we read every email. Write to support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days.

Getting started

How do I make a calendar?

Open My Calendars and tap New. Pick a type, set the date and names, and the calendar fills with ready-made messages. Edit any door in Settings (the gear).

When do doors open?

Each door unlocks at midnight, your local time, on its day. Doors before today stay open; future doors stay closed.

How do I write my own notes with help?

On a Wedding, Anniversary, Valentine's, Birthday, Baby, Retirement, Graduation, Vacation or Custom calendar, open Settings and choose the guided interview. Skip any question. On Android you can tap Regenerate for a fresh set of notes.

Sharing and opening calendars

How do I share a calendar?

Open the calendar, go to Settings, and tap Share. Send it as a file, show a QR code, or send a link.

How do I open one someone sent me?

Tap the file or link, or scan the QR code from My Calendars. Opening a shared calendar is always free.

The link isn't tappable.

Some messaging apps don't make these links tappable. On iPhone or iPad, copy the link, then use My Calendars › Paste Link. On Android, share the message text to Countdown Calendar. Or ask the sender for the file instead.

It says "Too big for a QR code."

Long calendars don't fit in a QR code. Share it as a file instead.

What does Gift lock do?

The person who receives a gift-locked calendar can't edit it or open doors early. It's a friendly lock to protect the surprise, not encryption: the file itself isn't secret, so don't put anything private in it.

Daily reminder

I'm not getting reminders.

The reminder is off until you turn it on in the calendar's Settings. Then allow notifications for Countdown Calendar when asked (or in your device's Settings app). Reminders come from your device, so they work without internet.

Purchases

What's free?

Christmas Advent and Hanukkah calendars, the Classic, Cats and Dogs door styles. Opening a calendar someone shares with you is always free. Sharing any calendar you can make is free, and so are all color themes. Everything else comes in three one-time unlocks: the Holiday Pack ($1.99), the Special Occasions Pack ($1.99) or Everything ($2.99), and each one includes editing.

I bought something and it isn't unlocked.

Use Restore Purchases on the unlock screen, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Apple and Google handle all payments and refunds; we never see your payment details. Refunds: Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com, Google via your Google Play order history.

Does Family Sharing work?

For Everything on iPhone and iPad, yes: everyone in your Apple Family Sharing group gets it on their own devices. If it doesn't show up, they tap Restore Purchases. The Holiday and Special Occasions Packs aren't shared. On Android, Google Play doesn't share in-app purchases with a family group, so each Google account buys its own. Family Sharing shares the purchase only, never your calendars.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase is one time. There are no ads.

Your data and a new phone

Where are my calendars stored?

Only on your device. We don't have a server or any copy of them.

I'm getting a new phone.

Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may bring your calendars along. To be sure, share each calendar to yourself as a file before you switch, then open the files on the new phone. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a calendar from My Calendars, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. We hold nothing about you to delete.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your device (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8), the app version (in Settings), and what happened. Please don't send us private messages from your calendars. We reply within 2 business days.
