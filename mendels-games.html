In development

Fetch the ball. Find the treat.

Mendel's Games is a free screen game for dogs, with two games: Fetch Chase and Treat Puzzle. Your dog plays with a tap of the nose. No ads, no accounts, no data, and it never uses the internet.

How it worksTell me when it's ready

Named for Mendel

Mendel's Games is named for Mendel, a dog who's the app's first tester. On your home screen, the app is simply called "Mendel".

Set it up, then play along

  1. Open Mendel. That's the name under the icon. The app opens to Mendel's Games with two big buttons.
  2. Pick a game: Fetch Chase or Treat Puzzle.
  3. Let your dog play. A nose tap anywhere on the play field counts, and the targets are big: about 150 points across on phones and 240 on tablets.
  4. Ten minutes of play. The time left shows at the top. Then the game gives way to a quiet night scene with a sleeping dog, the moon and stars. After a minute, a Play again button appears for a grown-up.
  5. To stop any time, tap ‹ Home at the top left.

Two games

  • Fetch Chase. A yellow ball rolls and bounces around a grassy field. Your dog nose-taps, and the dog on screen runs to the tap. A tap inside the soft white ring around the ball fetches it: a squeak, the fetch count goes up by one, and the ball pops up somewhere else, heading a new way.
  • Treat Puzzle. A bone hides under one of the cups (3 on phones, 4 on tablets), and the cups are shuffled. Nose-tap a cup to lift it. Empty cups stay up, and finding the bone rings a silver bell. Then a new round starts.

Sounds you control. The squeaker and the silver bell each have their own switch in Settings, with test buttons so you can hear them first. On iPhone and iPad, the sounds play even with the silent switch on.

Play it safe

  • Stay with your dog. Mendel's Games is for play time with you nearby, not a sitter. Stop if your dog seems frustrated or loses interest.
  • Protect your screen. Use a sturdy case and a tempered-glass screen protector, keep the device steady and out of chewing range, and wipe off nose prints afterwards.
  • Unplug the charger during play, so there's no cord to chew.
  • End with the real thing. A screen toy can't be caught for real, so finish with a real toy or a treat.

No accounts. No ads. No data. No internet.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app never uses the internet, and Zyzygy doesn't collect any data from it. The only things it keeps are your two sound switches, saved on the device.

Mendel's Games is a game for dogs, set up by their people. Like Kitty's Squeak & Splash, it isn't a kids' app.
