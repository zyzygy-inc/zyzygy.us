Five big, bouncy games in the colors dogs see best

Mendel's Games is a set of free screen games for dogs, for iPhone, iPad and Android. Lay the phone or tablet flat on the floor and let your dog nose or paw at the screen. No ads, no accounts, and no data collected.

The games

Mendel, a black dog, is the face of the app. Each game stars one of his dog friends. Here they are in menu order.

  1. Mendel's Tennis Ball. Starring Mendel. Nose or paw the ball and watch it bounce! One big, bright yellow ball keeps rolling around the screen, bouncing off the edges and into the corners. A tap sends it off a new way. It never leaves the screen.
  2. Baloo's Frisbee. Starring Baloo. Tap the frisbee and watch it zoom and bounce! One bright blue frisbee glides in gentle curves over pale sand and bounces off the edges. A nose or paw tap sends it in a new direction.
  3. Ruka's Lizard Chase. Starring Ruka. Catch the walking lizards. New ones keep coming! Up to seven yellow and light-blue lizards walk across dark sand on moving feet. Tap one and it's gone, and a new one walks in.
  4. Ollie's Chipmunk Hideaway. Starring Ollie. Chipmunks pop in and out of their holes. Catch one! Three golden chipmunks scurry around and slip into rocky caves, then come back out. A nose or paw tap tries to catch one.
  5. Whiskers' Squeaky Toy. Starring Whiskers, a gray miniature schnauzer. Catch the scurrying squeaky toy and hear it squeak! A little windup toy mouse zips around in quick bursts. Catch it and it hops away.

One speed. Nothing to set.

Every game has one speed and a set number of things on screen. There are no speed controls and no options to add more. Just open a game and set it down.

A nose tap can't close a game by accident: leaving takes a press-and-hold on the Home button, so a grown-up ends play. Each game has its own soft sound, and you can switch sounds off in Settings.

Made for dog eyes

Dogs see blues and yellows best and mix up red, orange and green. So every game uses big yellow or blue objects with strong contrast against a darker or paler background.

We'll be honest: not every dog takes to screen games. Some dogs play right away, and many need a little encouragement at first.

Play it safe

  • Stay with your dog. Screen play is meant to be supervised.
  • Phone or tablet flat on the floor, not held up or propped on a table edge.
  • Short rounds. End on a win, with a real toy or a treat your dog can actually catch.
  • A grown-up ends play.

Free, and we collect nothing

  • Free. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
  • No ads. Not one.
  • No accounts. No sign-in, no password, no email address.
  • No data collected. The app never uses the internet. Your sound settings stay on your device.
