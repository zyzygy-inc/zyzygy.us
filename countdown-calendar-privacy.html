Countdown Calendar privacy policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version: Countdown Calendar doesn't collect any personal information. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you make stays on your device until you choose to share it.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your calendars: titles, dates, door messages and animations, theme and door style, and any names or details you type in (for example partner names, years married, a birthday person's name and age, or a trip destination).
  • Things that never leave your device, even when you share: which doors you've opened, your guided-interview answers, your reminder settings, and display settings.
  • Your purchase status, read from Apple or Google.

Zyzygy, Inc. doesn't receive any of this. We have no server and no copy of your calendars.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

When the app uses the internet

The app works offline. It uses the internet only for in-app purchases, which Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handle under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. If you choose to share a calendar by message or email, that service carries it.

What a shared calendar contains

When you share a calendar (as a file, a link or a QR code), it contains the calendar itself: its type, title and dates, every door's message and animation, the theme and door styles, any names and details you entered, the "From" line, whether Gift lock is on, a random calendar ID and the date it was created. The link and QR code hold the same information in compressed form.

It does not contain your interview answers, which doors you've opened or your reminder settings.

Shared calendars aren't encrypted. Gift lock only stops the other person's app from editing it or opening doors early. Anyone who has the file, link or QR code could read the messages, so share it only with people you trust and don't put anything private in it.

Camera and notifications

  • Camera: used only when you tap Scan QR Code, to read a calendar's QR code. Nothing is saved or sent. The app asks first.
  • Notifications: the optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. It's off until you turn it on, and the app asks for permission then. On Android, the app also re-schedules reminders after your phone restarts. No push messages, no server.

The app doesn't use text-to-speech and doesn't print.

Backups

If your family uses Android's own backup, your phone or tablet may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup. On iPhone and iPad, your family's own iCloud Backup or computer backup may include it in the same way. Those backups belong to you and are covered by Google's or Apple's privacy policies. Zyzygy never receives them and can't see them.

Children

Countdown Calendar is a general-audience app for families. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Grown-ups make and share the calendars; kids just open the doors.

Deleting your data

Delete a calendar from My Calendars, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it 2 years after your question is resolved.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices change, we'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released. Questions: support@zyzygy.us. We answer by email only; we don't take questions by mail. Zyzygy, Inc., Goldthwaite, Texas.
