About Zyzygy

Zyzygy, Inc. is an independent software company based in Goldthwaite, Texas. Backed by over four decades of software engineering experience, we build dependable consumer apps and provide senior engineering services to businesses nationwide.

Our mission

We believe software should be reliable, transparent, and built to last. Every project we deliver is thoroughly tested, clearly documented, and written so the next engineer can maintain it with confidence. We set clear expectations, meet our commitments, and remain directly accountable to the people we serve.

What we do

  • Family-friendly apps. Small apps for families, plus screen games for cats and dogs. No accounts, no ads, and we don't collect your data. See our apps.
  • Software development for businesses. Remote, corp-to-corp work in Python, C and C++: fixed-price development work, a weekly retainer, or senior engineering by the hour. See our services.

How we work

  • Clear prices. We quote a fixed price or a weekly estimate after a short call about your project, before any work starts.
  • You can see the work. Progress comes as pull requests and demos you can check, not just status reports.
  • Modern tools, human review. Zyzygy uses AI coding assistants, and senior staff reviews every deliverable before it ships.
  • Straight answers. If a job is smaller than you think, or isn't a good fit, we'll say so.

Where we are

Zyzygy, Inc. is based in Goldthwaite, in Mills County, Texas. We work with clients remotely and bill as Zyzygy, Inc.
