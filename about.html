About Zyzygy

A small software company in Goldthwaite, Texas.

Jim's story

Jim Thornsberry has been writing software for over 40 years, most of it in Python, C and C++.

Today Jim works from Goldthwaite, in Mills County, Texas. Zyzygy, Inc. is the company he runs to do two things he enjoys: helping businesses with practical software, and making small apps for families.

Jim likes work that is careful and plain: code that does what it says, has tests, and is easy for the next person to read. He'll tell you if a job is smaller than you think, and he won't sell you something you don't need.
