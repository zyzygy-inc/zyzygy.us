iPhone or iPad

Please open this page on the iPhone or iPad itself.

  1. Get Apple's free TestFlight app, if not already installed.Get TestFlight
  2. Open your invite.Open the Punsapple Tree invite
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. The app will appear on your home screen.

Android phone or tablet

Please read all the steps below before you download.

Download the appabout 50 MB
  1. Tap the Download the app button above.
  2. Open the downloaded app file. Tap the download notification, or find it in your Files or Downloads app.
  3. A message pops up saying installing from this source isn't allowed. Tap Settings in that message.
  4. Switch on Allow from this source.
  5. Tap the back arrow.
  6. Tap Install.If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. This happens because it's a test version.
  7. Tap Open. That's it! 🎉
  8. Optional: you can switch the "Allow from this source" setting back off afterward.

Note: on older Android versions, you may instead find this under Settings, Security, Unknown sources.

🔒 Punsapple Tree has no ads, no accounts, and collects no data.
