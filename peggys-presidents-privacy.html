Peggy's Presidents privacy policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

We collect nothing.

The short version: Peggy's Presidents doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app works offline. The only thing it saves, your text-size choice, stays on your device.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your text-size choice (Regular or Large). That's the only thing the app saves.

It doesn't save which presidents or facts you've seen, or your quiz answers. Today's president comes from the date on your device.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. The app never sends it anywhere (your own device backup is covered under Backups below). Zyzygy, Inc. never receives it. We have no server and no copy of it.

All the presidents and facts are built into the app, so nothing is downloaded while you use it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ever, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.
  • No permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications.
  • No purchases or subscriptions: the app is free.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

When the app uses the internet

Peggy's Presidents works offline, and the app itself doesn't connect to the internet. You need a connection only to download the app or its updates, which the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) handles under their own privacy policies.

Who it's for

Peggy's Presidents is made for adults. It isn't directed at children. Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Backups

If you use your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone and iPad, or Google backup on Android), it may include the app's text-size choice. On Android, that one small settings file is the only thing the app allows into a backup. Those backups belong to you and are covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policies. Zyzygy never receives them and can't see them.

Deleting information

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it 2 years after your question is resolved.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices ever change, we'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released. Questions: support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days. We answer by email only; we don't take questions by mail. Zyzygy, Inc., Goldthwaite, Texas.
