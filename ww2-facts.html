World War II, one day at a time

WWII Facts brings you one World War II topic each day: a few short, plain facts about a battle, a person, an event, the home front, a place or an invention of the time. Free, private and no ads. In development for iPhone, iPad and Android.

How it worksQuestions? Support

Open it, and there's today's topic

  1. Today's Topic. The app opens straight to one topic, with today's date. You see its title, a postmark-style badge with the year and the kind of topic, a line saying when and where, and all of its facts (two to four) at once.
  2. Checked against. Under the facts, the app names the public records each one was checked against.
  3. Another topic, or All. Tap Another topic for the next one, or tap All to browse every topic in the order they come up.
  4. Regular or Large text. Choose it once in Settings. It works on top of your phone's own text size.
  5. Three gentle quiz questions a day, if you want them. Three answers to pick from, no timer and no score. Completely optional.

The topics follow a weekly rhythm: a battle, a person, an event, the home front, an interesting fact, technology and a place. They cover every part of the war, from Europe and the Pacific to Asia, North Africa, the Atlantic, the Eastern Front and the home fronts.

Four big buttons along the bottom (Today, All, Quiz and Settings) are all there is to learn. Nothing flashes, nothing moves on its own, and the app never sends notifications.

Told plainly, and with respect

Every fact is written in our own plain words and kept neutral: no glorifying, no graphic detail and no taking sides. Where a fact gives a number of lives lost, it says whose count it is, such as the U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum or the National Park Service.

Facts are checked against Wikidata (public-domain data) and U.S. government sources such as the National Archives, the Naval History and Heritage Command, the Library of Congress and the National Park Service.

A sample, from the Battle of Britain: a chain of radar stations along the coast gave British fighter controllers early warning of incoming aircraft.

Free, private and no ads

  • Free. Nothing to buy, no subscription, no in-app purchases.
  • No ads. Not one.
  • No accounts. No sign-in, no password, no email address.
  • No data collected. The app doesn't send anything to us or anyone else.
  • Works offline. Every topic is built into the app, so no internet is needed once it's installed.
