Reward Buddy support

Help for parents and grandparents. Email support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days.

The grown-up PIN

I forgot the PIN.

Tap Forgot PIN?, hold the button for 3 seconds, then answer a grown-up math question. You'll set a new PIN. Kids' points and walls are kept.

It says to try again later.

After 5 wrong PINs the grown-up area locks for 30 seconds, and longer with more wrong tries (up to 5 minutes).

What's behind the PIN?

Purchases, Restore Purchases, settings, chores and rewards, marking requests done, deleting, sharing and printing.

Points and rewards

Points stopped going up.

The points jar holds 1,000. When it's full, your child sees "Time to spend your points!" Spending some makes room again.

My child tapped a chore twice.

Take points back in the grown-up area: Children › adjust points. You can add a reason.

I didn't get the alert on my phone.

Alerts appear on the tablet only. Your child's info stays on the tablet, and Zyzygy never receives it, so there's nothing to send to your phone. Check the Requests list in the grown-up area.

The buddy isn't reading the joke aloud.

Check that Speak jokes aloud is on in Settings and that the tablet's volume is up. On Android, Reward Buddy only uses a voice installed on the tablet (never an online one); if there isn't one, the grown-up area's Settings shows how to download it. The joke always shows as big text too.

Locking the tablet to the app

How do I keep my child in the app?

iPad: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access), then triple-click the top or home button in Reward Buddy. The app shows a short guide. Android: turn on Lock to app in Settings; Android may ask you to confirm. To unpin, use Unlock in the grown-up area, or hold Back and Overview.

Printing and sharing certificates

How do I print a certificate?

Printing comes with Reward Buddy Plus. In the grown-up area, open a certificate and tap Print. It uses your tablet's own print dialog and a printer on your home network. Certificates look fine in black and white.

Can I send one to Grandma?

Yes. In the grown-up area, open a certificate and tap Share to send it as a picture. That's free.

Purchases

What does Plus include?

Your own chores and point values, your own grown-up rewards, four more buddies (dragon, unicorn, dinosaur, penguin), the Rainbow Stars certificate design, and printing. $2.99, one time. Renaming the 5 starter chores is free.

What are add-on packs?

Optional one-time purchases with extra jokes, certificate designs or dance songs, $1.99 each. Packs are separate from Plus, and you don't need Plus to buy one. Like everything else in the store, they sit behind the grown-up PIN.

My purchase didn't unlock.

In the grown-up area, open Plus & Packs and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the account you bought with. Apple and Google handle payments and refunds; we never see your payment details.

More than one tablet, or a new one

Can we use it on two tablets?

Yes, but each tablet is its own separate setup. Reward Buddy is made for a child to use one tablet to record chores and pick rewards, so kids, points and certificates don't move between devices and nothing syncs. If you add a second tablet, set your kids up there too. Plus comes back on the second tablet with Restore Purchases.

What does Family Sharing share?

Only the Plus purchase, on iPhone and iPad: people in your Apple Family Sharing group get Plus too. It never shares any data, such as kids' names, points or certificates. Packs aren't shared. On Android, purchases belong to the Google account that bought them and aren't shared with family.

Does anything ever leave the tablet?

Only when you choose it, and never to us:

  • A grown-up shares or prints a certificate.
  • Apple or Google handles a purchase or restore.
  • Your tablet's own backup includes the app's data, if you have backup turned on (see One tablet, by design).
  • On Android, jokes are read aloud only with voices installed on the tablet. If none is installed, the joke shows as text.

Zyzygy has no server and never receives your family's information.

We're getting a new tablet.

Purchases come back with Restore Purchases. Kids' points and walls live only on the old tablet; your device backup (iCloud or Google) may carry them over, but we can't promise it.

The app said it restored a backup.

Reward Buddy keeps a copy of its last good save on the tablet. If the main save is ever damaged, for example by a crash, the app loads that copy so points and the PIN aren't lost. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I delete my child's information?

In the grown-up area, delete the child (that removes their points, history and wall), or delete the app to remove everything.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your tablet (for example iPad 10th generation or Samsung Galaxy Tab), the app version, and what happened. Please don't include your child's full name or photos. We reply within 2 business days.
