Chores earn robot dance parties

Reward Buddy is a chore chart for kids 2 to 8, made for the family tablet. Kids earn points for chores, then spend them on rewards that cost you nothing. Now in development.

How it worksTell me when it's ready

Tap a chore. Earn points. Pick a reward.

  1. Your child taps their picture, then the chore they did, like Make bed or Brush teeth. Their buddy cheers and the points go up.
  2. They spend points on a reward:
    • a 10 to 15 second dance party from Robo the robot, who dances badly on purpose, to original music made inside the app
    • a joke read aloud by the tablet's built-in voice, with big text on screen too (knock-knock jokes are interactive)
    • a certificate, like "Great Job!"
  3. Bigger goals need a grown-up: rewards like "Pick dinner" and "Royalty for the Day" make a certificate and a little request for you to make it happen.

Every win goes on their wall

Certificates are saved on each child's wall of wins. Kids can star their favorites to keep them on top. Grown-ups can share a certificate as a picture, or print it with Reward Buddy Plus.

Simple for kids, in your control

  • Big buttons with a picture and a word on each, for kids who can't read yet.
  • A grown-up PIN protects purchases, settings, sharing and printing.
  • A points jar that fills up at 1,000, with a friendly "time to spend your points!" so nobody hoards forever.
  • No penalties. Kids only ever earn and celebrate.
  • Grown-up request alerts appear on this tablet, not on your phone, because your child's info stays on the tablet.
  • Calmer dances and no confetti when the tablet's Reduce Motion setting is on.

Free to use. Plus is $2.99, once.

Free: as many kids as you like, 5 starter chores (rename them to fit your family, free), dance parties, 30 jokes, certificates in the Classic Gold design, the "Pick dinner" and "Royalty for the Day" rewards, Robo the robot, and the wall of wins.

Free for the holidays: from December 1 to January 6, everyone also gets 8 Christmas jokes and 2 Christmas certificate designs, Snowflake Sparkle and Holly Jolly.

Reward Buddy Plus, $2.99 one time: make your own chores and point values, make your own grown-up rewards (like a trip to the park or baking cookies together), four more buddies (a dragon, a unicorn, a dinosaur and a penguin), the Rainbow Stars certificate design, and printing. Certificates you've already made keep their design.

Add-on packs, $1.99 each, one time: 2 joke packs, 2 certificate design packs (Space and Safari) and a Party Beats dance-song pack. You don't need Plus to buy a pack, and Plus doesn't include them. Every purchase sits behind the grown-up PIN. No subscriptions. No ads.

Your child's info stays on your tablet

No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. Kids' names, points and certificates stay on your family's tablet, and Zyzygy never receives them. You only need the internet to buy or restore an upgrade, and Apple or Google handles that.

Read the privacy policy

One tablet, by design.

No accounts. Your child's chores, points and certificates live on the tablet you set it up on, and nowhere else we can reach.

Reward Buddy has no accounts, and it stores information about your child only on the one tablet you set it up on. That's a deliberate privacy choice. Each tablet is its own setup, nothing syncs between devices, and Zyzygy never receives any of it. Family Sharing shares only the Plus purchase, never anyone's information.

App data stays on the device. If your family has Android's own backup turned on, the system may include it in your personal Google account backup so it can be restored to a new device. On iPhone and iPad, your family's own iCloud Backup or computer backup may include it in the same way. Zyzygy never receives or can see those backups.
