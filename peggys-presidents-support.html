Peggy's Presidents support

Help for you, and for anyone setting it up for someone else. Write to support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days.

Using the app

Where's today's president?

Right on the first screen. The app opens straight to the President of the Day.

How do I see another fact?

Tap Next fact. Each tap adds one more. When you've seen them all, the app says "That's every fact for today."

When does the president change?

Every day, after midnight on your device. The presidents go in order, one a day, and everyone sees the same president on the same date. After the last one, it starts again with different facts first.

How do I see a different president?

Tap Another president for the next one in order, or tap All at the bottom and tap any name. On a president's page, Previous president and Next president step through the list. Tap Today to come back.

Why are there 47 numbers but fewer names?

A president who served two terms that weren't back to back is listed once, with both numbers. Grover Cleveland, for example, appears as 22 & 24.

Where are the portraits?

Each president's portrait is at the top of his page.

How do I make the words bigger?

Tap Settings at the bottom, then Large under Text size. To go back, tap Regular. The app keeps your choice.

The words are still too small.

The app also uses your phone's own text size, so making text bigger on the whole phone or tablet helps here too. iPhone or iPad: Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size (for even bigger sizes, Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Larger Text). Android: open Settings and search for Font size. Menu names vary a little by device.

Do I have to take the quizzes?

No. Quizzes are completely optional and never pop up on their own. Tap Quiz for a fun quiz: three questions, each with four big answers. Play as many as you like. There's no timer and no score.

Does it send notifications?

No, never. Today's president is simply waiting when you open the app.

Is there a dark screen?

The app follows your phone's own light or dark setting.

Setting it up for someone else

Does it need an account, a password or an email address?

No. Just install it and open it.

Where's the app icon?

iPhone or iPad: on the Home Screen, or in the App Library (swipe left past the last Home Screen page). Touch and hold it and tap Add to Home Screen. Android: swipe up from the bottom of the Home screen to see all apps, then touch and hold the icon and slide it onto the Home screen.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and to get updates. After that, it works offline: no Wi-Fi or data plan needed.

Can I put it on more than one device?

Yes. It's free on each one.

About the facts

Where do the facts come from?

Public-domain data (Wikidata and the congress-legislators project), checked against U.S. government sources such as the National Archives, the Library of Congress, the National Park Service and the U.S. Congress. The sentences are our own plain-English writing. Credits are in the app under Settings › About.

Why are there no opinions about the presidents?

Because it's for everyone. We stick to objective facts, with no rankings and no commentary. Where a fact mentions a political party, it simply says which one, the same way for every president.

I think a fact is wrong.

Please tell us. Email support@zyzygy.us with the president's name and the fact. We'll check it against our sources and fix it in an update if needed.

Cost and privacy

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads.

Does it collect any information?

No. No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't use the internet at all. The only thing it saves is your Regular or Large choice, on your device.

How do I remove it?

Delete the app the usual way. That removes everything it stored on the device.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your phone or tablet (for example iPhone or Samsung Galaxy tablet), the app version if you can find it, and what happened. Someone else is welcome to write for you. We reply within 2 business days.
