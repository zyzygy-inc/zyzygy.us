Pun's Apple Tree privacy policy

We collect nothing.

The short version: Pun's Apple Tree doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app works offline.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ever, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of any kind.
  • The app doesn't ask for network access, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

The story, pictures and narration are all built into the app, so nothing is downloaded while you use it.

When the app uses the internet

It doesn't. You need a connection only to download the app or its updates, which the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) handles under their own privacy policies.

Deleting information

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it 2 years after your question is resolved.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices ever change, we'll update this page before that version is released. Questions: support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days. Zyzygy, Inc., Goldthwaite, Texas.
