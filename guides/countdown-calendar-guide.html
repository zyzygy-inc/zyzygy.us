User guide · iPhone, iPad and Android

Door a Day

A surprise picture every day. Count down to Christmas, Hanukkah, a wedding, a birthday or any day you choose, one little door at a time.

Pick your year, choose a theme, pick the content.

1. Make a new calendar

  1. Open Door a Day and tap New Calendar.
  2. Pick what you're counting down to: Christmas Advent, Hanukkah, Thanksgiving, Halloween, Easter, a birthday, a wedding, a vacation, and more.
  3. Pick your year and your number of doors. The app works out holiday dates for you: Easter, Thanksgiving (the fourth Thursday of November) and Hanukkah's 8 nights. A Christmas Advent calendar has 24 doors unless you change it.
  4. Give it a name, like "Emma's Birthday Countdown" or "Our Beach Trip," so you can find it again on your main screen.

2. Choose a theme

Each calendar has one theme. The theme decides how the doors look, the little animation when a door opens, and the surprise picture behind it. There's no separate door style to pick. The theme you're using is marked ✓ In use.

Themes include: Classic, Cats, Dogs, Fantasy Castle, Ocean, Space, Trains, Race Cars, Rockets, Rocket Ships, Dinosaurs, Trucks, Farm Animals, Western, Wedding Day, Vacation, End of School, School Start and Back to School.

More themes are on the way, including Bird, Anniversary Gifts, Fourth of July, Halloween Night and two Valentine themes. Hanukkah calendars always use their own look, with dreidels, gelt and latkes.

3. Pick the content

Next, check the boxes for what you'd like behind the doors: jokes, fun facts, riddles, kind things to do, thankful thoughts, quotes or verses, and little challenges. Pick as many as you like, and the app mixes them across your doors. You only see the choices that fit your calendar, and jokes match your theme. Bible verses (King James Version) are offered for Christmas and Easter calendars.

Then look over the doors the app made for you and tap to finish.

4. Make it your own

Editing is free. Tap Edit to change what's behind any door, write a personal note or a clue to a hidden treat, or swap doors around. Tap Done to save.

On your main screen, press and hold a calendar to Share, Duplicate, Rename or Delete it.

5. Open a door each day

  • Doors open in order, one each day. The next door to open gently pulses.
  • Tap it: a short animation plays, then the day's picture appears with its words underneath.
  • Doors for future days stay closed until their day comes. Opened doors stay open, so you can look again any time.

6. Share your calendar

Sharing is free for every calendar. Press and hold it and choose Share, then send it as a file (Messages, Mail or AirDrop), a QR code or a link. The person who gets it opens it in the free Door a Day app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

7. Add-on features

Christmas Advent and Hanukkah calendars are free. In Add-on features you can unlock more, once, with no account and no subscription:

  • Holiday Pack, $1.99: more holidays, like Halloween, Thanksgiving, New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day and Easter.
  • Special Occasions Pack, $1.99: weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, a new baby, retirement, graduation, vacations and any custom date.
  • Everything, $2.99: both packs and every theme.

Got a new phone? Tap Restore Purchases.

Your privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your calendars stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Questions? Email support@zyzygy.us or visit Door a Day support. We reply within 2 business days.
