Playing

What games are there?

Two: Fetch Chase, where your dog nose-taps a rolling yellow ball to fetch it, and Treat Puzzle, where your dog nose-taps cups to find the hidden bone.

Where is the app on my home screen?

Look for Mendel under the icon. In the App Store and Google Play it's listed as "Mendel's Dog Games".

How long does play last?

Ten minutes, with the time left shown at the top. Then a quiet night scene plays for a minute, and a Play again button appears. The 10 minutes can't be changed in 1.0.

How do I stop a game early?

Tap ‹ Home at the top left of the game. The rest screen has a Home button too.

My dog's nose taps don't seem to count.

The whole play field takes taps, and the target zones are big: about 150 points across on phones and 240 on tablets. In Fetch Chase, a tap inside the white ring around the ball fetches it; a tap farther away just sends the dog on screen running there. Wipe the screen clean and dry first, since touchscreens read taps poorly through water or smudges.

How many cups are in Treat Puzzle?

Three on phones and four on tablets.

How do I turn off the squeaker or the bell?

Tap ⚙ Settings on the Home screen. The squeaker and the silver bell each have their own switch, and Test squeak and Test bell let you hear them. Your choices are kept for next time.

I can't hear anything on my iPhone.

The sounds play even with the silent switch on, so check the volume buttons and that both switches in Settings are on.

My dog lost interest.

That's normal. Try the other game, keep play short, and finish with a real toy or a treat. Some dogs just aren't screen dogs.

Keeping the game on screen

How do I keep my dog in the game?

Mendel's Games doesn't lock the screen itself, so use your device's own lock. iPhone and iPad: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) and set a passcode, then start it from inside the app. Android: use screen pinning (often Settings › Security › App pinning; menus differ by maker).

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later. It works in portrait or landscape.

Will my dog scratch or chew the screen?

It can happen. Stay with your dog the whole time, use a sturdy case and a tempered-glass screen protector, keep the device steady and out of chewing range, and unplug the charger. Wipe off nose prints afterwards.

Cost and privacy

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Does it collect any information?

No. No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never uses the internet. It keeps only your two sound switches, on the device.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. It never goes online. You need a connection only to download it and its updates.

How do I remove it?

Delete the app the usual way. That removes everything it stored on the device.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your device (for example iPad 10th generation or iPhone 15), the app version (it's at the bottom of Settings, for example "Mendel's Games 1.0 (build 1)"), and what happened. A dog's name is optional. We reply within 2 business days.
