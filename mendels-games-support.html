Playing

What games are there?

Fetch-style chases and treat-finding puzzles, with a ball, a bone, a frisbee, a toy mouse, or a feather with a silver bell. [PLACEHOLDER: the game names and list from the spec]

How long does a game last?

Five minutes of play. Then a gentle 10-minute settle-down animation plays to wind things down. [PLACEHOLDER: whether the length can be changed, and how to end early]

Which tier should I pick?

Puppy, Adult or Senior. Start with the one that fits your dog, and try another if your dog seems bored or frustrated. [PLACEHOLDER: what each tier changes]

My dog's nose taps don't seem to count.

The tap zones are large, and bigger on iPad. Wipe the screen clean and dry first: touchscreens read taps poorly through water or smudges. [PLACEHOLDER: any catch-size or sensitivity setting in the app]

How do I turn off the squeaker or the bell?

Both can be muted. [PLACEHOLDER: where the mute switches are in the app]

What are holiday themes?

Seasonal looks for the games. [PLACEHOLDER: which themes ship in 1.0, and whether they're free]

My dog lost interest.

That's normal. Try a different game or tier, keep play short, and finish with a real toy or a treat. Some dogs just aren't screen dogs.

Keeping the game on screen

How do I keep my dog in the game?

Use your device's own lock. iPhone and iPad: turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) and set a passcode, then start it from inside the app. Android: use screen pinning (often Settings › Security › App pinning; menus differ by maker). [PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether the app has its own lock coach, like Kitty's Squeak & Splash]

Will my dog scratch or chew the screen?

It can happen. Stay with your dog the whole time, use a sturdy case and a tempered-glass screen protector, keep the device steady and out of chewing range, and unplug the charger. Wipe off nose prints afterwards.

Cost and privacy

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no subscription. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm there are no in-app purchases in 1.0]

Does it collect any information?

No. No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. [PLACEHOLDER: what it keeps on the device, from the code]

How do I remove it?

Delete the app the usual way. That removes everything it stored on the device.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your device (for example iPad 10th generation or iPhone 15), the app version, and what happened. A dog's name is optional. We reply within 2 business days.
