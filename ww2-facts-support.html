WWII Facts support

Help for you, and for anyone setting it up for someone else. Write to support@zyzygy.us. We reply within 2 business days.

Using the app

Where's today's topic?

Right on the first screen. The app opens straight to Today's Topic.

When does the topic change?

Every day, after midnight on your device. Everyone sees the same topic on the same date. After the last topic, the list starts again from the beginning.

How do I see a different topic?

Tap Another topic, or tap All at the bottom and tap any topic. On a topic's page, Previous topic and Next topic step through the list. Tap Back to today to come back.

What does "Checked against" mean?

It names the public records we checked that topic's facts against, such as the National Archives or the National Park Service.

How do I make the words bigger?

Tap Settings at the bottom, then Large under Text size. The app also uses your phone's own text size. iPhone or iPad: Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size. Android: open Settings and search for Font size.

Do I have to take the quiz?

No. The quiz is completely optional and never pops up on its own. Tap Quiz for three gentle questions a day, each with three answers. There's no timer and no score.

Does it send notifications?

No, never.

About the facts

Who writes the facts?

We do, in our own plain words. Each one is checked against Wikidata and U.S. government sources, listed under the facts.

I think a fact is wrong.

Please tell us. Email support@zyzygy.us with the topic and the fact. We'll check it against our sources and fix it in an update if needed.

Cost and privacy

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and to get updates. After that, it works offline.

Does it collect any information?

No. The only thing it saves is your Regular or Large choice, on your device.

Still stuck?

Email support@zyzygy.us. Tell us your phone or tablet, the app version if you can find it, and what happened. We reply within 2 business days.
