Sixteen games. Real cats. One very busy kitty.

Kitty's Fun Emporium is a set of screen games for cats, each one starring a real cat. Lay the tablet flat on the floor, lock it to the app, and let your cat do the hunting. No ads, ever. Coming late October.

See the gamesTell me when it's ready

Set it up, set it down, stand back

  1. Pick a game from the menu. Choose the speed and how long to play.
  2. Lock the screen. A short coach walks you through it the first time, so curious paws can't wander off into your email.
  3. Lay the tablet flat on the floor and let your cat hunt. Every catch gets a splash or a squeak and a happy little animation.
  4. Nap time. When the timer runs out, the critters head home and the app reminds you to finish with a real toy your cat can actually catch.

It's catch and release. Every critter gets away to play another day. Nobody gets hurt.

The games

Sixteen games, each starring cats from our family and friends.

Made for cat eyes. Cats see blues and yellows best, so the critters are bright and easy to spot. Sounds are soft, never bangs, and nothing flashes.

Paw-proof, as far as paws go

Cats are clever. Let's be cleverer.

  • A lock coach. The app walks you through your tablet's own lock: Guided Access on iPad and iPhone, screen pinning on Android. It shows a live "Locked" check so you know it worked. About 30 seconds the first time.
  • A grown-up exit. To leave a game, hold the dim paw in the top corner for 3 seconds with one finger, then tap the three numbers it shows. A cat lying across the screen just resets it.
  • Nothing for a paw to wander into. No settings, no rating pop-ups and no ads anywhere a paw can reach.
  • Belly flops ignored. Big touches like a belly, a tail or a full-on cat loaf don't count as catches.

We'll be honest: without the lock, a determined paw can still swipe home. With it, the only way out is yours.

Play it safe

  • Tablet flat on the floor, not held up or on a table edge. A tempered-glass screen protector and trimmed claws help.
  • Short and often. Most cats enjoy a few short sessions a day more than one long one. Stop if your cat seems frustrated.
  • End with a real catch. On-screen prey can't be caught for real, so finish with a toy they can grab, or a treat.
  • Stay nearby, and unplug the charger during play (cats chew cords). Not for cats with known light-triggered seizures.

We collect nothing

No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. No subscriptions. Your cat's name (if you add one) and catch totals stay on your tablet, and Zyzygy never receives them. The app itself never goes online.
