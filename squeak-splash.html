Fish that splash. Mice that squeak. One very busy cat.

Kitty's Squeak & Splash is two games for cats: Fishing and Mouse Chase. Lay the tablet flat on the floor, lock it to the app, and let your cat do the hunting. No ads, ever. In development.

How it worksTell me when it's ready

Set it up, set it down, stand back

  1. Pick a game: Fishing or Mouse Chase. Choose the speed, how many critters and how long to play.
  2. Lock the screen. A short coach walks you through it the first time, so curious paws can't wander off into your email.
  3. Lay the tablet flat on the floor and let your cat hunt. Every catch gets a splash or a squeak and a happy little animation.
  4. Nap time. When the timer runs out (10 minutes unless you pick another), the fish swim deep, the mice head home, and the app reminds you to finish with a real toy your cat can actually catch.

It's catch and release. Fish flip and swim off, mice do a dizzy spin and scamper into a hole. Nobody gets hurt, not even Squeakers.

Meet the cast

  • Goldie, a bright yellow goldfish who thinks she's faster than she is.
  • Bluegill Bob, a blue fish with a sunny yellow belly. Bob has been caught before. Bob will be caught again.
  • Squeakers, a classic mouse, and Cheddar, a cream-colored mouse with a very twitchy tail. Yes, the tail counts.
  • Plus koi, a frog, a crab and a dragonfly that skims the pond, and a moonlit Night Pond for evening hunts.

Made for cat eyes. Cats see blues and yellows best and can't tell red from green, so the prey are bright yellow against deep blue water and slate-gray floors.

Moves like the real thing. Mice dart, freeze, twitch and peek out from under the rug. Fish glide, flick and dive. The mix keeps changing so your cat doesn't get bored. Sounds are soft: little squeaks and splashes, never bangs, and nothing flashes.

Paw-proof, as far as paws go

Cats are clever. Let's be cleverer.

  • A lock coach. The app walks you through your tablet's own lock: Guided Access on iPad and iPhone, screen pinning on Android. It shows a live "Locked" check so you know it worked. About 30 seconds the first time.
  • A grown-up exit. To leave a game, hold the dim paw in the top corner for 3 seconds with one finger, then tap the three numbers it shows. A cat lying across the screen just resets it.
  • Nothing to buy mid-game. No store, no settings, no rating pop-ups and no ads anywhere a paw can reach. Your cat can't go shopping.
  • Belly flops ignored. Big touches like a belly, a tail or a full-on cat loaf don't count as catches.

We'll be honest: without the lock, a determined paw can still swipe home. With it, the only way out is yours.

Play it safe

  • Tablet flat on the floor, not held up or on a table edge. A tempered-glass screen protector and trimmed claws help.
  • Short and often. Most cats enjoy a few short sessions a day more than one long one. Stop if your cat seems frustrated.
  • End with a real catch. On-screen prey can't be caught for real, so finish with a toy they can grab, or a treat.
  • Stay nearby, and unplug the charger during play (cats chew cords). Not for cats with known light-triggered seizures.

We collect nothing

No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. No subscriptions. Your cat's name (if you add one) and catch totals stay on your tablet, and Zyzygy never receives them. The app itself never goes online. Buying or restoring a purchase goes through Apple or Google.
