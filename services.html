Software consulting for businesses

Zyzygy, Inc. provides remote, corp-to-corp software consulting in Python, C and C++. The work is led by Jim Thornsberry, who has been writing software for over 40 years.

Ask for a quoteHow engagements work

Corp-to-corp only

Zyzygy, Inc. contracts directly with your company, business to business. Your company signs an agreement with Zyzygy, Inc., and Zyzygy, Inc. invoices your company for the work. We don't take W-2 employment or payroll positions.

What we do

  • Python automation and tools. Scripts, internal tools, scheduled jobs, data pipelines (ETL), and small integrations and webhooks.
  • Python back ends. FastAPI, Django and Flask services: new features, error handling, sign-in and access, tests, and basic monitoring.
  • C and C++ systems and performance. Libraries and tools, profiling, memory problems, and measurable speed-ups. Embedded-adjacent work when the scope is clear.
  • Legacy modernization. A thin wrapper, foreign-function interface, or service boundary around older C or C++ code, so it works with newer systems without a full rewrite.
  • Code audits. A written review of your codebase with a ranked list of what to fix first.
  • Tests, CI and refactoring. Unit and integration tests, a build pipeline that passes again, and cleanup of existing code.

How engagements work

  1. Tell us the outcome you need. A bug, a feature, a migration, a slow program, or a repository you want reviewed.
  2. Short scope call. We talk through the work, then send a fixed price before the work starts, or a weekly estimate for ongoing help.
  3. Remote delivery. You see progress as pull requests and demos.
  4. One invoice from Zyzygy, Inc. Corp-to-corp, to your company.

Ways to engage

  • Fixed price development work. A defined job at a set price, agreed before the work starts.
  • Weekly retainer. A set amount of time reserved for you each week, with your work at the front of the line.
  • Senior engineering by the hour. Python, or C and C++ systems work, for teams that want an extra pair of hands.

Is this a good fit?

We do our best work on existing codebases and well-scoped projects. Larger new products work best when someone on your side owns the product decisions. We don't take on-site-only roles, roles that require an active security clearance, or work that breaks the law or another site's terms of service.
