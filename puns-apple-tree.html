The Princess & the Punsapple Tree

A picture book full of puns, read aloud

Punsapple Tree brings the picture book The Princess & the Punsapple Tree, by Don Giffels and illustrated by Dolores O'Callaghan, to iPhone, iPad and Android. No ads, no accounts, and no data collected.

31 pages: the cover, a dedication, 28 story pages and a page about the creators.

About the book The games

The book

The Princess & the Punsapple Tree was written by Don Giffels and illustrated by Dolores O'Callaghan (© 2013).

The pun words are printed in red, just like on the page.

Read the book

  • Read to Me. Each page is read aloud, and the page turns on its own. Tap the pause apple at the top right any time.
  • Read Myself. No voice and no auto-turn. Go at your own pace with the back and forward apples at the bottom.

Extras: three games

When the story is done, there are three games to play. Pick Games on the front page.

  • Punsapple Picking. A gentle game at the apple tree. Tap an apple and it falls. The princess, all smiles in her purple dress, catches it and takes a crunchy bite, and you hear the pun read aloud. She tucks the apple in her basket and walks back, ready for the next one. No pun repeats, and the game ends when the tree is empty.
  • Find the Apples. Pick one of four black-and-white scenes (the town, the lakeside, the castle or the field) from the little pictures, or tap Surprise me for one you haven't done yet. Look for the hidden black-and-white apples. Each one you tap turns red, and a counter shows how many you've found out of the total. Find them all and the scene gets a green check.
  • Elephant Memory Game. Elephants remember everything! Can you? Eight windows with blue shutters open on the back of the castle to show the King, Princess Gala, the elephant, a bouquet, a bag of coins, the sun, a framed picture and an apple, in a different spot every game. You get 10 seconds to remember where everything is. Then the shutters close, and a voice asks you one at a time, like "Where is the King?" Tap the window you think it's behind. A gentle memory game for kids.

Illustrations by Dolores O'Callaghan.

Private and no ads

  • No ads. Not one.
  • No accounts. No sign-in, no password, no email address.
  • No data collected. The app doesn't send anything to us or anyone else.
  • Works offline. Once it's installed, no internet is needed.
