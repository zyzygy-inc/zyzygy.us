The Princess & the Punsapple Tree

A picture book full of puns, read aloud

Pun's Apple Tree brings the picture book The Princess & the Punsapple Tree, by Don Giffels and illustrated by Delores O'Callaghan, to iPhone, iPad and Android. No ads, no accounts, and no data collected.

How it works

Four ways to enjoy the app

  1. Read to Me. Each page is read aloud, and the page turns on its own. Pause any time.
  2. Read Myself. No voice and no auto-turn. Go at your own pace with Back and Next.
  3. Punsapple Picking. A little game in the orchard. Tap an apple on the tree, and the princess catches it in her basket. Each apple brings a pun from the story, with the pun word in red.
  4. The elephant memory game. A memory game with objects from the book.

Every picture is one of Delores's original paintings from the printed book. The pun words are printed in red, just like on the page.

The book

The Princess & the Punsapple Tree was written by Don Giffels and illustrated by Delores O'Callaghan (© 2013).

Private and no ads

  • No ads. Not one.
  • No accounts. No sign-in, no password, no email address.
  • No data collected. The app doesn't send anything to us or anyone else.
  • Works offline. Once it's installed, no internet is needed.
