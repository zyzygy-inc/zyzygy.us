A surprise picture every day

Door a Day counts down to Christmas, Hanukkah, a wedding, a birthday or any day you choose. Choose a theme, and each day opens a new surprise picture from it. Coming late November to iPhone, iPad and Android.

How it works

Pick your year, choose a theme, pick the content.

15 surprise themes

Choose a theme, and every day brings a new picture from it. There are 15 themes, including Trains, Dogs, Cowboy, Wedding and Hanukkah. Themes like "fetch a ball" and "find a treat" may come later.

Holiday and special occasion expansion packs planned.

Countdowns for every occasion

  • Holidays: Christmas Advent, Hanukkah (8 nights, dates worked out from the Hebrew calendar), Halloween, Thanksgiving, Easter, New Year's Eve
  • Love: Valentine's Day, Wedding, Anniversary
  • Life events: Birthday, Baby on the Way, Retirement, Graduation, Vacation
  • Custom: any date, any title

Add names and details, like the couple's names or the birthday child's age, and the messages fill them in for you. Pick from 21 color themes.

Doors they'll love

Pick how the doors look and open, for the whole calendar or one door at a time.

  • Classic: a paneled door with a bow.
  • Cats: cat-face doors that open like a cat flap.
  • Dogs: 12 dog faces, from a golden retriever to a puppy in a Santa hat. The doors open like a pet flap.
  • Race Cars: a fun new look on every door, from a race car and a taxi to a hot rod. The doors swing open like a car door.
  • Dinosaurs: a fun new look on every door: speckled eggs crack open and little dinosaurs peek out.
  • Rocket Ships: a fun new look on every door: a porthole with a view of Earth, Mars, Saturn or the stars pops open for a launch.
  • Trucks: a fun new look on every door: a dump bed tips up or a garage door rolls up on a fire truck, a digger and more.
  • Farm Animals: barn doors swing open to a cow, chicken, pig, sheep or horse.

When a door opens, the style's characters join in for a second or two: a cat bats a paw, a puppy wags, a farm animal says hello, a dinosaur stomps and roars, a car zooms past, a rocket lifts off, or a truck tips out confetti.

Switch on fun facts & jokes to fill the doors with kid-friendly facts for that style. Your own messages stay unless you choose to replace them. Classic, Cats and Dogs are free. The other door styles come with Everything ($2.99).

Don't know what to write? We'll help.

For Wedding, Anniversary, Valentine's Day, Birthday, Baby, Retirement, Graduation, Vacation and Custom calendars, a guided interview asks a few simple questions. Skip any you like. Then the app writes a personal note for every door, and you can change anything afterward.

  • iPhone and iPad: the questions come on one page.
  • Android: one question per screen. Preview the notes and tap Regenerate for a fresh mix.

It all happens on your device, with no internet and no AI service. Your answers stay on your device and aren't included when you share the calendar.

Share it. They open it free.

  • As a file: send it by Messages, Mail or AirDrop, or save it to Files. Tapping the file opens it in the app.
  • As a QR code: they scan it in the app. (If a calendar is too big for a QR code, the app tells you to share it as a file.)
  • As a link: a link that opens the calendar in the app. Some messaging apps don't make these links tappable. On iPhone, copy it and use Paste Link; on Android, share the text to the app.

Anyone you share with opens it in the free app, whatever the calendar type. The same calendar works on iPhone, iPad and Android, including the family iPad the grandkids use.

Gift lock: turn it on and add a "From" line, like "From Grandma ❤️". The person you send it to can't edit it or open doors early. It's a friendly lock to keep the surprise, not encryption.

A nudge when a new door is ready

Turn on the optional daily reminder and your device lets you know when today's door is ready. It's off until you turn it on, it's set for 9 AM unless you change it, and it's a notification scheduled on your own device, with no internet needed.

Free to start. No ads. No subscriptions.

Free: Christmas Advent and Hanukkah calendars, the Classic, Cats and Dogs door styles. Opening a calendar someone shares with you is always free. Sharing any calendar you can make is free too. All color themes are free.

Three one-time unlocks for everything else:

  • Holiday Pack, $1.99: Halloween, Thanksgiving, New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day and Easter
  • Special Occasions Pack, $1.99: Wedding, Anniversary, Birthday, Baby on the Way, Retirement, Graduation, Vacation and Custom (any date, any title)
  • Everything, $2.99 (best value): both packs plus every paid door style (Race Cars, Dinosaurs, Rocket Ships, Trucks and Farm Animals)

Every paid unlock includes editing: write your own door messages and questions.

On iPhone and iPad, Everything works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets it too. The Holiday and Special Occasions Packs aren't shared. On Android, Google Play doesn't share in-app purchases with a family group, so each Google account buys its own.

Buy once and it's yours. No ads, ever, and no subscriptions.

Private by design

No account and no sign-in. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your calendars and messages stay on your device until you choose to share them. The camera is used only to scan a calendar's QR code.
